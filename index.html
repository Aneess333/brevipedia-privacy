Brevipedia Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy covers the Brevipedia app for Android. Brevipedia doesn't collect, store on a server, sell or share your personal data.

What the app collects

Nothing. There is no account or sign-in, and the app has no analytics or advertising. It doesn't ask for your location, contacts, camera or files, and it doesn't use the internet. Everything you read is stored inside the app.

What stays on your device

Your best quiz and game scores, and the steps you tick on career roadmaps, are saved on your device so they're there next time. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. They aren't included in Android's app backups. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Links to other apps and websites

Some buttons open other apps, such as your browser for a government website, Maps for a place, or your phone app for an emergency number. Those apps and sites have their own privacy policies, which we don't control.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Government information

Brevipedia is an independent app. It doesn't represent any government or political body, and you can't apply for documents through it.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes what it does with data, for example by adding ads, we'll update this page and its date before that version is released.
